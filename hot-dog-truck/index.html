Hot Dog Food Truck Catering in Tucson

Tucson has a deep love for the hot dog — especially the iconic Sonoran dog. Our hot dog truck brings that tradition to your event in style.

The hot dog is one of America's great communal foods — and in Tucson, it carries special significance thanks to the beloved Sonoran hot dog tradition brought north from Sonora, Mexico. Our hot dog truck honors that heritage while serving the classic American versions that everyone from kids to executives love.

The Sonoran Hot Dog — Tucson's Signature

The Sonoran dog is a Tucson institution: a bacon-wrapped hot dog nestled in a soft bolillo-style bun and topped with pinto beans, diced tomatoes, onions, crema, mustard, and mayonnaise. It's hearty, flavorful, and unmistakably Tucson. We serve it with pride at every event.

🌭 Menu highlight: Our Sonoran-style hot dogs are consistently the first item to run out at every event we attend. For events over 100 guests, we recommend ordering extra Sonoran dog servings in your package.

Hot Dog Menu Options

  • Classic American dog — mustard, ketchup, relish, onion
  • Sonoran dog — bacon-wrapped, beans, tomato, crema, onion
  • Chili cheese dog — house chili, shredded cheddar, diced onion
  • Chicago dog — yellow mustard, relish, onion, tomato, pickle, sport peppers, celery salt (no ketchup — we follow the rules)
  • Loaded dog of the day — rotating specialty topping combination
