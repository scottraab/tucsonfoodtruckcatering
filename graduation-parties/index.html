Food Truck Catering for Graduation Parties in Tucson

Graduation is a milestone worth celebrating with exceptional food. Let us handle the catering while you celebrate the grad — no cooking, no stress, just good food and great memories.

Graduation season in Tucson is one of the year's most exciting stretches — University of Arizona commencement, high school graduations across TUSD, MUSD, Vail Unified, and Amphitheater Unified all happen within a few weeks of each other in May. Families gather from across the state and country, and feeding that crowd well matters.

University of Arizona Graduation Catering

UA graduation weekend is our single most requested event period of the year. Families planning a backyard party, park gathering, or private venue reception after the commencement ceremony call us months in advance to lock in a date. If you're planning a UA graduation party, reach out as early as possible — May weekends fill very fast.

🎓 Booking deadline: For May graduation parties — especially UA graduation weekend — we strongly recommend booking by March. April inquiries may find limited availability. Don't wait.

High School Graduation Catering

We serve graduation parties for students from across the Tucson area, including:

  • Tucson Unified: Rincon, Cholla, Amphitheater, Flowing Wells, and others
  • Marana Unified: Mountain View, Marana High
  • Vail Unified: Cienega High School
  • Sahuarita Unified: Sahuarita and Desert Sky high schools
  • Private schools: Salpointe, Basis, Canyon del Oro, and others

Typical Graduation Party Setup

Most graduation parties run 2–4 hours with a guest count between 40 and 150. We arrive, set up, serve continuously during your event window, and leave the space spotless. Many families add our dessert truck for a sweet ending to the celebration.
