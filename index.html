Common Questions

Answers Before You Ask

How far in advance should I book a food truck?
We recommend booking at least 3–4 weeks in advance for most events, and 6–8 weeks for weddings or large corporate events. That said, if you have a last-minute need, always reach out — we do our best to accommodate shorter timelines when our schedule allows.
How much does it cost to hire a food truck in Tucson?
Pricing depends on guest count, event duration, location, and menu selections. Most events in the 50–150 guest range fall between $600 and $1,800. We provide free, transparent quotes with no pressure — just fill out our form and we'll send you exact numbers within a few hours.
Do you serve all of Tucson including the suburbs?
Yes — we serve all of Pima County including Marana, Vail, Sahuarita, Catalina Foothills, Oro Valley, Green Valley, and beyond. There may be a small travel fee for locations more than 25 miles from central Tucson, which we'll always disclose upfront in your quote.
Do you need electricity or water hookups at the venue?
Our truck is fully self-contained for most events. For extended events (4+ hours) we prefer access to power, but we can discuss the specifics of your venue when you reach out. We've served parks, parking lots, private properties, and everything in between.
