Southern Arizona's Local Food Truck Catering Service

We're rooted in Tucson, dedicated to its communities, and passionate about making every event we serve memorable.

Built for Tucson. Built on Food.

Tucson Food Truck Catering exists because Southern Arizona's events deserve better than generic, impersonal catering. We're a local operation — deeply familiar with the communities we serve, from Marana's growing subdivisions to South Tucson's tight-knit neighborhoods to the Catalina Foothills' private estates.

Our network of food trucks serves hot dogs, burgers, burritos, specialty coffee, and sweet treats — the foods that Southern Arizona's diverse communities genuinely love and celebrate with. We don't try to be everything. We do a focused menu exceptionally well, for events of every size and occasion.

Why We Do This

Food brings people together. That sounds like a cliché, but stand near a food truck at a neighborhood block party or a company picnic and watch what happens — people gather, conversations start, strangers become neighbors. The truck creates a social nucleus that no catering table with aluminum trays can replicate.

We've seen the moment at hundreds of events when the truck arrives and the whole atmosphere changes. That's what we're here for.

📍 Locally operated: We live and work in Pima County. When you book with us, you're supporting a local business that knows your neighborhood, respects your community, and shows up the way a neighbor would — on time, prepared, and genuinely invested in your event going well.

Our Commitment to Tucson's Communities

Tucson was designated the first UNESCO City of Gastronomy in the United States — a recognition of its 4,000 years of continuous agricultural tradition and its extraordinary food culture. We take that designation seriously. Every menu item we serve is built to honor Tucson's food identity: the Sonoran hot dog tradition, the hearty burrito culture, the Sonoran Desert ingredients and flavors that make this city's food scene unlike anywhere else in the country.

We serve the full diversity of Tucson's communities — from quinceañeras in South Tucson to corporate events in Marana to HOA gatherings in Vail — with equal care, equal quality, and equal respect for the traditions that make each community unique.
